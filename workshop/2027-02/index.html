How do we stop AI agents from doing bad things,
and get them doing good work?

AI agents are moving from demos into real work, and in 2027 the hard questions are about trust. Will the biggest failures come from bad data, or from agents escaping their sandboxes? Who checks the AI-generated output piling up in every organization? Can we secure agents with fundamental solutions we already know how to build?

This showcase brings industry and academic speakers together with the faculty and students of the Data, Agents and Processes Lab (DAPLab) at Columbia to share how to prevent the bad things agents can do and enable the productivity they promise: talks, a panel, and posters and demos.

The event is invite-only, for leaders from industry and government. See our past workshops.
