--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} A blog post: the same page header as every other page, then the article in the reading style first made for the CacheBack post (files/css/blog/article.css). On wide screens the list of posts has its own column left of the article, with an empty one of the same width on the right, so the article stays centered and nothing overlaps. "Hide posts" blanks the column without moving anything (remembered in localStorage). On narrower screens "All posts" opens the list above the article. There is no separate index; /blog forwards to the newest post.
